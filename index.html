Ways to keep it down

  • Write the budget down before you shop and stick to it.
  • Set a per-person limit for gifts, and agree it with family.
  • Try Secret Santa for extended family or friends.
  • Plan the food shop and check what you already have.
  • Avoid putting Christmas on credit if you can.
