Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Who we are

whatitcosts.ie is run from Ireland. If you have a question about this policy or about your data, email whatitcosts@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing you type into the budget planner or the Christmas Plan Builder is collected or sent to us. The calculations run in your browser, on your device.

If you unlock paid access, the page remembers that by saving an "unlocked" flag in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and we can't see it. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Render. Like most hosts, Render may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, to keep the service secure and running. See Render's privacy policy.

Payments

Paid access is a one-off purchase processed by Stripe. Stripe handles your card details and acts as its own data controller for them, under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your card number.

In our Stripe account we can see purchase details such as your name, email address and the amount paid. We use these to give you access, to help if something goes wrong, and to keep the records the law requires. Our legal basis is providing what you bought and meeting our legal obligations. We keep purchase records for as long as Irish tax law requires, usually six years.

Links to other sites

We link to Instagram, MABS and an RTÉ news article. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply when you visit them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have it corrected
  • have it erased
  • restrict how we use it
  • receive it in a portable format
  • object to how we use it

To use any of these rights, email whatitcosts@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date.
